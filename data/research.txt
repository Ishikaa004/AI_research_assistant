Generative AI is a branch of artificial intelligence that can generate
new content such as text, images, audio, and code.

Large Language Models are neural networks trained on large amounts of
text data. They can understand and generate human-like language.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation, or RAG, combines information retrieval
with language generation. Instead of relying only on knowledge learned
during model training, a RAG system retrieves relevant information from
external documents and provides that information to the language model.

Embeddings are numerical representations of text. They allow a system
to compare the semantic meaning of different pieces of text.

A vector database stores these numerical representations and allows
efficient similarity search.
